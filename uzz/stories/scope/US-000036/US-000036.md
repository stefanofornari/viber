## US-000036 — Conversation History Maintenance in Scoping Phase

### User Story

```
AS A Product Owner
I WANT the conversation history to be maintained within the scoping phase chat
SO THAT I can refer back to earlier parts of our discussion during the scoping process
```

### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Conversation history is preserved during scoping session
  Given I am in a scoping phase session
  When I exchange multiple messages with ScopeAgent
  Then the full conversation history is maintained in the correct order
  And I can refer back to earlier parts of our discussion

Scenario: Message ordering is maintained
  Given I am in a scoping phase session
  When I send a prompt followed by receiving a reply
  Then the prompt appears before the reply in the conversation history
  And each message is correctly scoped to the scoping phase chat

Scenario: History scope isolation
  Given I have completed a scoping phase and started a regular chat
  When I examine the conversation history
  Then the scoping phase messages are contained within their designated chat
  And regular chat messages are contained within their designated chat
  And there is no mixing of messages between different chats
```
