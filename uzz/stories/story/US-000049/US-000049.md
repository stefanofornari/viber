## US-000049 — Conversation History Maintenance in Story Generation Phase

### User Story

```
AS A Product Owner
I WANT the conversation history to be maintained within the story generation phase chat
SO THAT I can refer back to earlier parts of our discussion during the story generation process
```

### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Conversation history is preserved during story generation session
  Given I am in a story generation phase session
  When I exchange multiple messages with StoryActor
  Then the full conversation history is maintained in the correct order
  And I can refer back to earlier parts of our discussion

Scenario: Message ordering is maintained
  Given I am in a story generation phase session
  When I send a prompt followed by receiving a reply
  Then the prompt appears before the reply in the conversation history
  And each message is correctly scoped to the story generation phase chat

Scenario: History scope isolation
  Given I have completed a story generation phase and started a regular chat
  When I examine the conversation history
  Then the story generation phase messages are contained within their designated chat
  And regular chat messages are contained within their designated chat
  And there is no mixing of messages between different chats
```
